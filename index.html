HOW TO PLAY
  1. Each player has a shuffled 1–9 board.
  2. BREAK secretly picks a closed square on their board. BLOCK guesses its position there.
  3. Same square: BLOCK! Stays closed. Different: BREAK! Opens. The turn ends either way.
  4. TOP: first player BREAKS. BOT: second player BREAKS. The other BLOCKS. Both turns = 1 inning.
  5. Complete a row, column, or diagonal to score its numbers. Each number scores once.
  6. Up to 9 innings. Higher score wins; ties draw. Ends early if the result cannot change.
  7. 2P: 60 seconds per pick. Timeout picks a random closed square.
