HOW TO PLAY
  1. BREAK secretly picks one closed square on their own board.
  2. BLOCK guesses the same square.
  3. Same square: BLOCK! The square stays closed.
  4. Different square: BREAK! The square opens.
  5. Complete a line to score its numbers. Each number scores only once.
  6. After 9 turns each, the highest score wins. The match ends early if the result cannot change.
